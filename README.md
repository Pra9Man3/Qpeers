# Qpeers

### Meaningful connections. Shared professional journeys.

**Qpeers** is a dating and networking platform designed for qualified
professionals and the wider professional community --- including
Chartered Accountants (CA), Company Secretaries (CS), Cost and
Management Accountants (CMA), and other eligible professional groups.

The idea is simple: make it easier for people with shared professional
backgrounds, interests, and life experiences to discover one another and
build genuine connections.

> **From networking and friendship to dating and meaningful
> relationships.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Why Qpeers?

Generic social and dating platforms may not always provide the
professional context or community familiarity that some people are
looking for.

Qpeers aims to create a focused space where members can connect with
people who understand similar academic, qualification, and career
journeys.

The platform is being designed around:

-   **Community-focused access** --- for eligible professional groups
    and, where supported, their students.
-   **Credential-aware verification** --- a process to establish
    professional or student status.
-   **Connections with purpose** --- friendship, networking, shared
    interests, dating, and long-term relationships.
-   **Meaningful discovery** --- profiles that highlight interests,
    preferences, professional background, and what a member is looking
    for.
-   **Privacy and safety** --- user controls, reporting, blocking, and
    responsible handling of personal information.

The following features describe the product direction and may change as

## Who Is Qpeers For?

The initial community focus includes:

-   Chartered Accountants (CA)
-   Company Secretaries (CS)
-   Cost and Management Accountants (CMA)
-   Eligible students pursuing these qualifications
-   Other professional communities that may be included in the future

## Product Philosophy

Qpeers is **not intended to be just another swipe-based dating app**.

It is being shaped around a broader idea:

**People → Conversations → Connections → Friendship / Networking /
Dating → Potential Relationships**

Members should be able to choose the kind of connection they are open
to. Dating is one option, not an assumption.

## Privacy and Safety

Trust is central to the product. The intended design includes:

-   Keeping sensitive credential and identity documents private
-   Showing only an appropriate verification status on public profiles
-   Avoiding public display of date of birth, registration numbers,
    membership numbers, or identity documents
-   Giving members control over profile visibility
-   Providing blocking and reporting mechanisms
-   Supporting account and data deletion processes

Professional or student verification, where implemented, confirms only
the relevant credential or status. It does not guarantee a member's
character, intentions, or personal safety.
